# Mission 4: Stay on Track

## 🎯 Your mission

Program the Cutebot to follow a black line using its line-tracking sensors.

## 🧠 Today's idea

Today we're learning about:

**Iteration and sensor-based decisions**

---

## 🔎 Explore

Try this:

1. Place the Cutebot on black and white surfaces and note left/right sensor outputs.
2. Drive slowly along a line and watch which sensor changes.

### What happens if...

- the robot goes faster? 
- the motors have different speeds? 
- the line is wide or broken?

---

## 🛠️ Design

### ⭐ Challenge 1

Follow a short straight line reliably.

### ⭐⭐ Challenge 2

Handle a corner without leaving the track.

### ⭐⭐⭐ Challenge 3

Design a new track with straights and corners for another group to try.

---

## 💡 Invent

You've completed the mission! Now see if you can:

- Reduce wobble by tuning motor speeds.
- Make the robot follow a broken or dashed line.
- Improve reliability on different surfaces.

There is no single right answer.

---

## 🧠 Think

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What would you change?
- What could you try next?

---

## 🏁 Mission complete!

I can...

- [ ] Read left and right line sensors.
- [ ] Keep the robot on a line through a corner.
- [ ] Tune motor speeds to improve tracking.
