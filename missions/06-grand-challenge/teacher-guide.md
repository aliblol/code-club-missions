# Teacher Guide — CuteBot Grand Challenge

Session goals
- Encourage decomposition, debugging and optimisation; support open-ended creativity.

Setup
- Provide a delivery course and optional scoring/timing equipment.

Hints for volunteers
- Focus on prompting students to explain their choices and trade-offs.
- Help groups test and iterate rapidly.

Assessment
- Can the student complete the delivery reliably and explain improvements?

Notes
- Collect best starter and finished MakeCode projects into `code/`.
