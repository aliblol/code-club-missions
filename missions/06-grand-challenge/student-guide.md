# Mission 6: CuteBot Grand Challenge

## 🎯 Your mission

Design and implement an autonomous delivery robot that navigates to a delivery zone.

## 🧠 Today's idea

Today we're learning about:

**Algorithms, decomposition, debugging and optimisation**

---

## 🔎 Explore

Try this:

1. Test movement, sensors and individual behaviours (line-follow, avoid, turn).
2. Identify the hardest part of the course and sketch a plan.

### What happens if...

- you try to make the robot faster? 
- you add more obstacles? 
- multiple robots run the same course?

---

## 🛠️ Design

### ⭐ Challenge 1

Complete a basic delivery and signal arrival.

### ⭐⭐ Challenge 2

Complete the delivery while avoiding obstacles reliably.

### ⭐⭐⭐ Challenge 3

Optimise your solution for speed, reliability, or simplicity.

---

## 💡 Invent

You've completed the mission! Now see if you can:

- Add timing, scoring or multiple delivery points.
- Coordinate two robots for cooperative delivery.
- Make the robot adapt to changes in the course.

There is no single right answer.

---

## 🧠 Think

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What would you change?
- What could you try next?

---
