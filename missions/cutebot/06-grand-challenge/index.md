# Mission 6 — CuteBot Grand Challenge

The grand challenge brings together everything students have learned throughout the Cutebot project. They combine navigation, sensing, planning, and debugging to create a complete solution that solves a more complex problem.

This final mission encourages creativity, resilience, and reflection. Students are expected to design, test, and improve a project that demonstrates both technical skill and a clear understanding of how robot behaviour is created through code.
