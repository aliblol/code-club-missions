# Teacher Guide — Mission name

## Learning goals

- Introduce the main concept or skill.
- Encourage experimentation and debugging.
- Help students explain what changed and why.

## Setup

- Prepare devices, materials, or equipment needed for the task.
- Make sure students have the correct starter files or code blocks.
- Set out challenge materials, if relevant.

## Suggested flow

1. Introduce the mission and the learning goal.
2. Let students explore the task in pairs or groups.
3. Prompt them to test different ideas before finalising a solution.
4. Reflect on what worked, what failed, and why.

## Hints for volunteers

- Ask students to predict before they test.
- Encourage them to change one thing at a time.
- Use questions rather than answers when students get stuck.

## Assessment

- Can students complete the core challenge?
- Can they explain the idea they are testing?
- Can they improve or extend their solution?

## Notes

- Add starter and completed examples in the `code/` folder.
- Keep the student language short, clear, and accessible.
- Use the same concept names in the teacher guide and student guide.
