# Mission 1 — Wake Up Your Cutebot

This mission introduces students to the basics of controlling a Cutebot. They learn how to send simple movement commands, change values such as speed and duration, and notice how small adjustments affect the robot's behaviour.

The focus is on playful experimentation: students test, compare, and refine how the robot moves before moving on to more complex challenges. This is a strong first mission for building confidence with coding, sequencing, and debugging.
