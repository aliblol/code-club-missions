# Teacher Guide — Stay on Track

Session goals
- Teach iteration and sensor-based decisions; show feedback loops.

Setup
- Prepare a black-line track with at least one corner.

Hints for volunteers
- Suggest small motor speed changes to reduce wobble.
- Encourage students to test and adjust sensor thresholds.

Assessment
- Can the student keep the robot on the line through a corner?

Notes
- Save example MakeCode projects in `code/` for reference.
