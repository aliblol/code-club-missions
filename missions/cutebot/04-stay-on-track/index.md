# Mission 4 — Stay on Track

Students tackle the challenge of keeping a robot moving in a controlled and repeatable way. This mission focuses on accuracy, correction, and understanding how small changes can keep the robot on course.

By testing and adjusting their code, students learn that successful robot behaviour is often the result of repeated improvement, careful observation, and debugging rather than one perfect attempt.
