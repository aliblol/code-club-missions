# Mission 5 — Robot Rescue

This mission turns the robot into a helper in a more realistic scenario. Students need to combine movement, decision-making, and problem-solving as they guide the Cutebot through a rescue-inspired task.

It is designed to bring together earlier learning in a more open-ended challenge, encouraging students to plan, test, and improve a solution that must work reliably under changing conditions.
