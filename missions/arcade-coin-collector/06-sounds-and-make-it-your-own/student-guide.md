# Lesson 6: Sounds and Make It Your Own

## 🎯 Your mission

Add sound feedback to game events, then customise the game however you like. The game should still be winnable — beyond that, anything goes.

---

## 🧠 Today's idea

Today we're learning about:

**Cause and effect**

This means we are learning how every sound is triggered by an event — collecting a coin *causes* a ding, getting hit *causes* a sad sound. Good feedback tells the player what just happened.

---

## 🔎 Explore

Open the starter `main.ts` (the full Coin Collector game from Lesson 5).

1. Where in MakeCode can you play a sound? Look in the **Music** section.
2. What is the difference between `play sound` and `play sound until done`? Try both in the collect event.
3. Can you find a block to loop background music throughout the game?

### What happens if...

- you use `play sound until done` for collecting — does anything feel different?
- you add background music — does it keep playing after the game ends?
- you speed up the coin spawn interval to `500 ms`?

---

## 🛠️ Design

### ⭐ Challenge 1 — Collect sound

Add a sound inside the coin collect event. Look in the **Music** section.

### ⭐⭐ Challenge 2 — Hit sound

Add a different sound to the enemy hit event — choose one that feels right for getting hit.

### ⭐⭐⭐ Challenge 3 — Make it your own

The game is yours to change. Pick at least one idea below, or invent your own:

- Change the theme — redesign all three sprites with a new setting (space, underwater, jungle)
- Add a second enemy at a different speed
- Speed up over time — create a variable for enemy speed that increases at score 5 and 10
- Add a power-up — a special coin worth 5 points that appears occasionally

The only rule: the game must still be winnable.

---

## 💡 Invent

You've completed the mission! Now see if you can:

- Add a background melody that loops, and stops cleanly when the game ends
- Make something happen when the player reaches a score of 5
- Share your game with someone else and watch them play

There is no single right answer.
