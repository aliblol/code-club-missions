**Game:** Coin Collector  
**Time:** 20 minutes  
**Concepts:** Overlap events, score changes

## What we're building today

Today students make the coin collectible. When the player touches it, the score goes up, the old coin disappears, and a new one appears somewhere else.

By the end, collecting a coin changes the score and gives the player another coin to chase.
