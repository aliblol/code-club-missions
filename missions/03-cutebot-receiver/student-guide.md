# Mission 3: Don't Crash!

## 🎯 Your mission

Make the Cutebot stop or avoid obstacles using a distance sensor.

## 🧠 Today's idea

Today we're learning about:

**Conditions and sensors**

---

## 🔎 Explore

Try this:

1. Test the ultrasonic sensor at 5cm, 10cm and 20cm — note the readings.
2. Drive the robot toward an object and watch the sensor values.

### What happens if...

- the robot is moving quickly? 
- the object is small or angled? 
- you set the threshold too low?

---

## 🛠️ Design

### ⭐ Challenge 1

Stop when the distance is less than 10 cm.

### ⭐⭐ Challenge 2

When an obstacle is detected, reverse and turn to avoid it.

### ⭐⭐⭐ Challenge 3

Map a short obstacle course and make the robot navigate around objects.

---

## 💡 Invent

You've completed the mission! Now see if you can:

- Use different reactions depending on how close the object is.
- Combine line-following and obstacle avoidance.
- Add a short alarm or LED pattern when avoidance happens.

There is no single right answer.

---

## 🧠 Think

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What would you change?
- What could you try next?

---

## 🏁 Mission complete!

I can...

- [ ] Read distance sensor values and compare them to a threshold.
- [ ] Make decisions using `if` blocks.
- [ ] Avoid simple obstacles reliably.
